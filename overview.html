The problem

Credit cards come as fixed packages.

Most cards bundle features you didn't choose, look the same as everyone else's, and you often don't know if you'll be accepted until you've applied.

North tests a different idea: check first, then build a card around your own priorities, with the value of every perk shown in pounds before you commit.

Who it's for

Four kinds of customer, one builder.

Credit builders

Getting a first card or making a fresh start.

Everyday spenders

Want money back on groceries, bills and the usual.

Frequent travellers

Want miles, lounges and cheaper spending abroad.

Cost spreaders

Have a big purchase coming up or a balance to move.

What it does

From questions to a card in your wallet.

  1. Check eligibilitySix quick questions and a simulated soft search show your likely credit limit.
  2. Pick 3 perksCashback, travel, 0% offers or credit building, with their value per year after fees.
  3. Design itColour, pattern, material, finish, orientation and your name, with a live preview.
  4. Review and applyEverything in one place: limit, fees and representative APR.
  5. Use it nowYour finished card drops straight into your phone wallet.
